# IDK

AI research project at the intersection of spirituality and science — using AI to explore
the deepest questions about existence, consciousness, and the universe. Named for the honest
answer to those questions, and for RV 10.129's own refrain: *perhaps even He does not know.*

Current thread: **the Vedic corpus as sound, language, and knowledge-system.**

## Layout

```
texts/            the corpus (see texts/SOURCES.md for full provenance of every file)
  vedas/          all four Vedas — Rigveda in 5 scholarly editions (VedaWeb/Cologne),
                  accented Taittirīya + Vājasaneyi YV, Sāmaveda, both Atharvaveda recensions
  upanishads/     20+ Upaniṣads, five with Śaṅkara's commentary
  puranas/        14 major Purāṇas (GRETIL critical editions)
  shastras/       the knowledge systems: yoga, āyurveda, jyotiṣa/math, music, rasaśāstra
                  (alchemy/metallurgy), language science (Pāṇini, Yāska), statecraft
docs/
  METHODOLOGY.md  ← START HERE: how we unravel — six Vedāṅgas × modern tools,
                  the six-pass reading protocol, the claims ledger
sanskrit/         sound-first Sanskrit learning path
sound/            the sound research: framework doc, recitation audio, working analyzer
findings/         dated research notes — every claim with evidence and open questions
```

## Method in one line
Read the primary source in Sanskrit, on its own terms (sound first), with every commentary
we hold and every measurement we can make — and log every claim as attested, supported, or open.
